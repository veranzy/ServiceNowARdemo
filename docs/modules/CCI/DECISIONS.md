# CCI — decisions

Numbers 1–3 are carried over from the old MDM module. Invoicing (former INV) had no module decisions;
it follows D2 (system of record), D12 (never automatic) and D15 (billing hierarchy).

| # | Question | Decision | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the contract record whether late payment interest applies? | Yes: three fields on the contract, in a *Late payment interest (LPI)* form section. | 2026-11-04 |
| 2 | Where does the customer name come from? | From the platform `core_company` table (D26). `x_ar_customer.company` (Reference, mandatory, display field) → `core_company`; name dot-walked from `company.name`, not stored. Only the name comes from `core_company`; UEN, address and other details stay on `x_ar_customer`. | 2026-11-12 |
| 3 | Who creates the customer record for a new customer on AR-i? | AR-i only records the customer details on the request (transaction data). The processing officer creates the `x_ar_customer` record with *Link to customer* and must choose its `core_company` record. | 2026-11-12 |
| 4 | Customer record tabs | Billing hierarchy tab removed; usage and purpose to revisit ([issue #6](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/6)). Billing schedules are no longer listed on the customer, only on contracts. Customer and contract records show **every tab** (no More menu). | 2026-11-16 |
| 5 | Contract category and Contract ID | Field `contract_type` is labelled **Contract category** with choices **Contract / No contract** (No contract = the former no-contract shell). Every record gets a read-only system **Contract ID** (CTR0001001 …). **Contract number** stays the customer's number: mandatory for Contract, optional for No contract (shows the Contract ID when empty). A customer can have several No contract records (D15 amended). | 2026-11-16 |
| 6 | Billing schedule under a contract | **Contract** is mandatory on a billing schedule; the customer follows the contract (read-only). | 2026-11-16 |
| 7 | Daily invoice check | D12 amended: a daily scheduled job creates each billing period's **draft** invoice 5 working days (Mon–Fri) before its invoice date, linked to the schedule, the contract and the original AR-i case and line (shown on the case's new **Invoices** tab, with a work note). Draft → issued stays manual by the AR team (*Issue invoice*). *Raise invoice* retired. Demo: mock data only (amended same day: no demo button; ARQ0001009 loads with the job's draft). Until the forecast logic is specified, the job uses the schedule's existing period dates. | 2026-11-16 |
| 8 | 1st invoice on close; schedule from the 2nd invoice | **Every AR-i** set to **Closed complete** generates its **1st invoice** as a **draft** from the AR-i form (one-off: its only invoice; recurring: the first period). Closing is blocked until the case is linked to a customer and contract. The invoice has a new **Approval date**, set manually by the AR team. For a recurring AR-i, AR then creates the **billing schedule by hand on the contract** (*New billing schedule*) for the **2nd invoice onwards** and picks the AR-i case (*From AR request*); the daily job's invoices then show under the case and the contract. Recurring AR-i cases show a read-only **Billing schedule** tab (the contract's schedules). *Create invoice* and *Set up billing schedule* on billing lines are retired. | 2026-11-16 |
| 9 | 1st invoice review, approval date, billing cycle | The **1st invoice is always reviewed manually** (too hard to assess automatically). Its **Invoice date** is suggested from the AR-i's indicated start (recurring: line start; one-off: preferred invoice date; else today), kept even if earlier than today, and AR sets the separate **Approval date** by hand, which can be earlier or later than the invoice date. **Every invoice** needs an Approval date before *Issue invoice* (the button saves the form first). The **2nd invoice onwards** follows the contract **billing cycle** stated by the project team: *New billing schedule* defaults the first billing date to the next 1st / 15th, and a schedule on a 1st / 15th contract must start on that day (monthly from it). Custom cycle (*Others*): AR sets the first billing date. Answers open questions 14 and 15. | 2026-11-16 |
| 10 | Contract owner (E8.5, [#8](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/8)) | New contract field **Contract owner** (`contract_owners`, List → `sys_user`, several owners, project team users; on the Contract section after Department). Owners can pick the contract on an AR-ix even when it belongs to another department (AR-ix decision 22). Maintained by AR in the workspace (D22). | 2026-11-17 |
