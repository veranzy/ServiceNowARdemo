# Decision log

Cross-module decisions for the ServiceNow AR build. Module-specific decisions live in each
module's `docs/modules/<CODE>/DECISIONS.md`. The *Source* column may use former module codes
(ARF, GIR, MDM, INV, CSH); `docs/modules/README.md` maps them to today's modules.

| # | Date | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-11-01 | All tables are custom tables in the scoped app (no CSM `customer_account`, `core_company` or Contract Management `ast_contract`). **Amended by D26:** the customer references `core_company` for its name. | Owner |
| D2 | 2026-11-01 | The app is the system of record for invoices. | Owner |
| D3 | 2026-11-01 | Two roles: project team uses the portal only; AR team uses the workspace only. | Owner |
| D4 | 2026-11-01 | Project team raises the AR form as a record producer; it creates an AR request case worked in the workspace. | Owner |
| D5 | 2026-11-01 | **No auto-matching.** Deposits are always matched to invoices manually by the AR team. | Owner (BSU) |
| D6 | 2026-11-01 | Bank statements arrive as CSV exports, imported once a day for the previous day, deposits only. | Owner (BSU) |
| D7 | 2026-11-01 | **Money received is only ever a BSU deposit.** No separate payments table or "payment received" step: cash application matches BSU deposits (Bank statements table) to invoices. | Owner |
| D8 | 2026-11-01 | Change requests reference **Build notes IDs** (`<ID> - <change>`). IDs are stable and listed in `docs/BUILD_NOTES.md`. | Owner |
| D9 | 2026-11-02 | The portal offers **16 AR forms, AR-i … AR-xvi**; each is a record producer. AR-i (New AR request) is the general request; "first" and "recurring" are not separate types (see D13). | Owner (ARF) |
| D10 | 2026-11-02 | Customer documents (signed contract/PO, ACRA registration, billing contact & address form) are **filed on the customer once** and reused by later requests. | Owner (ARF) |
| D11 | 2026-11-02 | There are **11 operational departments**; a customer can have a **different billing contact per department**. | Owner (ARF) |
| D12 | 2026-11-02 | Recurring billing is requested **once** (as a recurring billing line); AR runs it from a **billing schedule** and raises each invoice manually. | Owner (ARF) |
| D13 | 2026-11-02, **amended 2026-11-12** | ~~Every AR request has billing lines: one-off, or recurring over a from–to date range (frequency Monthly by default). One request can mix both.~~ **Amended:** an AR-i is **one billing event**. The requester fills **Invoice details** (no billing lines on the form); submit creates **one** billing line: recurring (monthly, from the preferred invoice date to the contract end date) when the contract type is Recurring, otherwise one-off. Billing line items (optional) break the invoice down. | Owner (ARF, PT-05; amended ARF 31) |
| D14 | 2026-11-02 | The customer is **searched first**; a new customer is added only when not found. A "first request" is simply a request for a new customer. | Owner (ARF, PT-05) |
| D15 | 2026-11-02 | **Billing is contract level**: customer › contract › billing lines › invoices. Billing with no contract is filed under the customer's **no-contract shell** (a contract record of type shell, one per customer + department), so the hierarchy always holds. | Owner (ARF, PT-05) |
| D16 | 2026-11-02 | The **bill-to person is held on the contract** (or shell). The department billing contact is only the default used to prefill it. | Owner (ARF, PT-05) |
| D17 | 2026-11-03 | The AR workspace follows the **Horizon Design System** workspace templates (tabbed app shell, list page template, Record page template with ribbon and contextual side bar). The portal keeps its own look. | Owner (UIX) |
| D18 | 2026-11-03 | The workspace uses the **ServiceNow default workspace theme** (no branded theme), and every record page has a **Record history** tab backed by the audit log (Audit on for every `x_ar_` table). | Owner (UIX) |
| D19 | 2026-11-10 | Every AR form starts with the shared **General information** section: requester name, department, email (from SSO, always read-only) and a **CC list** of emails whose people can view the case on the portal. | Owner (ARF, GIR) |
| D20 | 2026-11-10 | **AR-ix GIRO authorisation** is a case like AR-i. If the contract isn't on file, AR processes its AR-i first and then links the case to the contract. ~~GIRO is recorded in a GIRO authorisation table~~ (replaced by D21 on 2026-11-11). | Owner (GIR) |
| D21 | 2026-11-11 | **Contract › AR-ix.** There is no GIRO table: when an AR-ix case is approved and linked, the contract's **GIRO status** becomes Active with that case as the **active GIRO case**. A contract has **at most one active GIRO**; a second Add GIRO is blocked until a Remove GIRO case ends the current one (replace-instead-of-block is open, issue #2). **Amended 2026-11-12:** AR sets GIRO status and active case on the contract by hand from the AR-ix case, then completes the case (AR-ix decision 11); wording to reconcile in the next major release, [issue #4](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/4). | Owner (GIR) |
| D22 | 2026-11-12 | **AR forms and customer / contract updates are separate on purpose.** A form is a request and never changes master data by itself; AR updates customers and contracts in the workspace. Forms only read master data (e.g. AR-i shows the contract's GIRO status read-only; GIRO changes only through AR-ix). **Note 2026-11-12:** AR now updates GIRO on the contract by hand from an AR-ix request (AR-ix decision 11); see [issue #4](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/4). | Owner (ARF, GIR) |
| D23 | 2026-11-12 | **Customer details on AR-i** (agency type, V@G, V@G sub-BU, POC name and email) are transaction data on the request and are **replicated to the customer master** when AR links the customer. For a customer already on file the form shows the master values **read-only** (follows D22). | Owner (ARF) |
| D24 | 2026-11-12 | **Contract billing terms live on the contract.** Contract type (Recurring / Ad-hoc), reference and PO numbers, billing cycle, preferred invoice date, payment terms, proration (1st / last month amounts) and type of billing (with the C / D / E bulk upload) are contract fields. AR-i shows an existing contract read-only and collects them only for a new contract; Link to customer copies them. Proration applies to every recurring line under the contract. (AR-i decisions 27–30.) |
| D25 | 2026-11-12 | **Billing line items** carry GST: GST amount = amount (w/o GST) × the GST rate (9%, a system property), total = amount + GST, both calculated. Amounts entered on AR-i (amount billed per period, item amounts) are **before GST**. | Owner (ARF 32–33) |
| D26 | 2026-11-12 | **Customer name comes from `core_company`** (amends D1). `x_ar_customer` stays a custom table but has a mandatory reference `company` → platform `core_company`; the customer name is dot-walked from `company.name` and not stored on our table. `company` is the display field. UEN, address and the other customer details stay on `x_ar_customer`. One customer per company. AR-i keeps the requested customer details as transaction data on the request; the processing officer creates the customer record (Link to customer) and must pick its company. | Owner (MDM 2) |
| D27 | 2026-11-12 | **Module map.** One module per AR form number (AR-i, AR-ix, …; AR-ii … AR-xvi when defined) plus **AR-00** for the parts every form shares; **CCI** = customers, contracts and invoices (was MDM + INV); **BSU** = bank statement upload and cash application (was BSU + CSH); **UIX** unchanged. Each module is a folder `docs/modules/<CODE>/` (spec, decisions, open questions, change log). Module decision numbers are carried over (ARF → AR-i, GIR → AR-ix, MDM → CCI); build-note IDs are unchanged. Git rules: `docs/GIT_WORKFLOW.md`. | Owner |
