| 23 | Billing lines are the PT-43 rows; Generate invoice (owner, 2026-11-27) | Amends **decision 8** (1st invoice on Closed complete) and **D13**. (1) The AR-i form details stay on the case (`x_ar_request`); billing lines, billing schedules and invoices are separate tables, each linked to the contract, so the same records show on the case and on the contract (two entry points, same behaviour). (2) On submit, **each row of the AR-i Billing line items table (PT-43) is saved as a billing line** (`x_ar_request_line`), linked three ways: AR-i case, contract, line. No billing line when *Require billing line item?* = No. `x_ar_request_item` is retired. (3) A billing line only **prints an invoice line** on the invoice PDF: PT-43 fields only (item number, amount w/o GST, GST, GST amount, total, late payment interest, account code, cost center); **no logic** on it (no one-off / recurring type, dates, frequency, invoice or schedule link). One-off / recurring is the case's (contract type). (4) **Closed complete no longer generates the 1st invoice** and no longer needs the Link section. New UI action **Generate invoice** beside Save on every AR-i case, any state: once Link Customer Reference and Link Contract Reference are set, each click drafts **one** invoice for the case (amount = total of the billing lines with GST, or the Invoice details amount w/o GST when there are none; invoice date today; prints the case's billing lines). AR reviews it, sets the Approval date (decision 9) and issues it. (5) Billing schedules: to be redesigned (owner will describe them). Interim: *From AR request* fills the empty schedule fields from the case (invoice description, amount per period, monthly, contract start and end dates), and AR can change them; the daily invoice check is unchanged. | 2026-11-27 |
