# BSU — decisions

| # | Question | Decision | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | File formats | CSV bank export only. Each bank has its own column layout; the upload uses the template of the account's bank. | 2026-11-01 |
| 2 | What happens after upload | Deposits are saved to the **Bank statements** table for later manual review and matching. **No auto-matching, ever** (see D5). | 2026-11-01 |
| 3 | Which lines | **Deposits (credits) only.** Withdrawals in the file are skipped and counted. | 2026-11-01 |
| 4 | Banks | Mock aliases for Singapore's three largest banks: **Marina Bank**, **Orchard Bank**, **Union Straits Bank**, each with a different CSV layout. | 2026-11-01 |
| 5 | Upload cadence | One upload per bank account per day, for the **previous day** (statement date defaults to yesterday). | 2026-11-01 |
| 6 | Deposit ID | ~~Each deposit is tagged `DEP-<bank code><last 4 of account>-<YYMMDD>-<NNN>`, e.g. `DEP-MB4521-261031-001`.~~ Replaced by decision 18. | 2026-11-01 |
| 7 | Bank accounts | Bank account master with bank, account number, name, **department** and **account type** (Revenue, Deposit, Operating). | 2026-11-01 |
| 8 | Controls | Keep it simple for now: no duplicate, balance or continuity checks. | 2026-11-01 |
| 9 | Routing by department / account type | **No routing.** All deposits go to one AR queue (Cash application). Department and account type are labels for reference only. | 2026-11-01 |
| 10 | Non-customer deposits (interest, refunds, transfers) | **Leave them unmatched.** No special status; they stay in the to-match list and are handled outside the app. | 2026-11-01 |
| 11 | Matching to Collections cases (D29) | Built 2026-11-17: the invoice finder also lists open Collections cases (AR-iii, AR-iv, AR-v), matched against the case's **Request value** (`amount`, owner: "Collection will use the amount field too"). The allocation sets `request` instead of `invoice`. Matching **never changes the case state** (no automation); AR closes the case by hand. Existing labels (*Find invoice*, *Add invoice…*) kept. No demo collection cases until AR-iii is defined. | 2026-11-17 |
| 12 | Upload record (D32) | **The upload is an RITM**: one per bank account per statement day, raised by AR's direct upload in the workspace; the deposits are its related Bank statements records. Number prefix `RITM` (was `BSU`). | 2026-11-18 |
| 13 | Bank accounts and collections | Each department has its own bank accounts, and certain collections arrive in certain accounts. Bank account gets **Collection types** (AR-iii / AR-iv / AR-v). In the finder, a case whose type the deposit's account receives **ranks higher**; nothing is hidden (D5). Collection cases are also found by their own references (AR-iv Reference Number, AR-v G56 receipt, case and IB / WEU numbers). | 2026-11-18 |
| 14 | Finder labels | *Find invoice or case…*, *Add invoice or case…*, *Search invoice / case no., customer, reference or amount* (owner: proposed wording). Answers open question 6. | 2026-11-18 |
| 15 | Invoices and cases together | One deposit can be split across invoices and Collections cases (owner, 2026-11-18). Answers open question 5. | 2026-11-18 |
| 16 | Simpler BSU page | The **BSU** rail item opens the **Statement uploads** list: all upload RITMs. **New** on the list opens the upload form (bank account, date, CSV, preview, save), and saving opens the new RITM. The statement-day checklist, the *Recent uploads* card and the landing-page *Statements for …* card are retired; tracking upload completeness is decided later. | 2026-11-18 |
| 17 | Bank account master list | **Bank name** and **Bank account number** are separate (fields and list columns). **Bank account name** describes the sub-account type under the department, so the department understands it (e.g. Project deposits, Retention and deposits). Field names unchanged. | 2026-11-18 |
| 18 | Payment modes and Deposit ID (replaces 6) | AR defines **payment modes** under each bank account: *Payment mode*, *Transaction ID* (the value in the bank statement's transaction-code column), *Prefix*, *Suffix*, *Active*. Each deposit's transaction ID picks the payment mode, and the **Deposit ID = Prefix-DDMMYY-Suffix**, DDMMYY being the deposit (value) date. **IDs may repeat** (owner): deposits of one payment mode on one day share the ID, so record URLs use the record, not the Deposit ID. A line with **no matching payment mode is saved with a fallback ID** `UNK-DDMMYY-XX` and shows *No payment mode* in the preview. Each mock bank CSV gets a transaction-code column (Marina *Transaction Code*, Orchard *Txn Type*, Union Straits *Transaction ID*). All sample deposits regenerated in the new format. | 2026-11-18 |
| 19 | Payment mode without Transaction ID (amends 18) | The payment mode has no separate *Transaction ID*: the **Payment mode** value is the code in the statement's transaction column (e.g. GIRO, FAST, CHQ, TT), matched exactly, not case-sensitive. The deposit keeps its *Transaction ID* (what the bank sent). | 2026-11-18 |
| 20 | Account code and cost centre on payment modes | Payment modes get **Account code** and **Cost centre** (optional references to the CCI master lists), as form fields and list columns, each with the tooltip *To check: why is it needed?* until the purpose is confirmed (open question 11). | 2026-11-18 |
| 21 | Matching in the standard workspace (replaces the Cash application page) | The **Cash application** page (`CSH-01`) is retired with its rail icon, score cards, invoice finder and export; old links open *BSU deposits to match*. Matching happens on standard record pages (owner): **from a deposit and from an invoice or Collections case**. Each has a **Suggested** tab (ranked candidates with a *Match* button per row; suggestion only, D5) and an **Allocations** tab with **New** (a *New allocation* form: deposit, invoice or Collection case, amount, reason). Partial and multi-invoice payments are several allocations; *Match* and an empty amount default to the smaller of what is left on the deposit and what is due. The landing card *BSU deposits to match* is removed; the list is the queue. | 2026-11-18 |
| 22 | Allocation value and delta | The allocation is a **child item of both** the BSU deposit and the invoice or Collections case. Its own field **Allocation value** (was labelled *Amount*) is unique to the allocation; the deposit, invoice and case amounts are fixed (single source of truth) and never change. Each Allocations tab (deposit, invoice, *BSU deposits matched* on a case) shows the record's **Amount**, the sum of its **Allocation value**s and the **Delta** (Amount − Allocation value), to support refunds (deposit side) and offsetting (invoice / case side) when there is over- or underpayment. **Match** on a Suggested row no longer saves at once: it opens *New allocation* with Allocation value prefilled (smaller of the deposit's unmatched amount and the amount due) for AR to confirm or change. No extra read-only figures on the form (owner). | 2026-11-18 |
| 23 | Bank statement lines list (AR Team user journey) | After **Save** on a new statement upload, AR lands on the **bank statement lines of that upload** (BSU deposits list filtered to the RITM). The BSU deposit lists (*BSU deposits to match*, *All BSU deposits*, in the *Bank statements* menu group) show **RITM, Value date, Deposit ID, Bank name, Bank account** (account number), then Payer, Reference, Amount, Unmatched, Match, **sorted by RITM (newest first) › date › Deposit ID › bank name › bank account**. Matching stays on the line's record (decisions 21, 22). Built first so AR2 and AR10 (reconciliation, refund, underpayment) can be discussed with the users. | 2026-11-23 |
| 24 | Bank statement list opens the upload | In the BSU deposit lists, clicking a row (Deposit ID) opens its **upload (RITM)** record. The upload record shows its lines first: tab **BSU deposits** (all lines in that upload) comes **before Details**, then Record history. AR opens a line from that tab to see its details and match it. RITM and Deposit ID in the list are plain text (the row is the link). | 2026-11-23 |
