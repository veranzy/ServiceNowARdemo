# AR-ix — decisions

Numbers are carried over from the old GIR module (before the 2026-11-12 restructure, D27).

| # | Question | Decision | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is "directbankauth" | **GIRO** (direct debit authorisation). | 2026-11-10 |
| 2 | Case | AR-ix is a record producer on the AR request case table (`x_ar_request`, `ar_form = AR-ix`), worked in the workspace like AR-i. | 2026-11-10 |
| 3 | Contract | The requester picks the customer, then an **existing contract**, or says it is **not listed** and gives the contract / tenancy agreement / reference number. An existing contract links the case at submit. Otherwise the AR officer **processes the contract's AR-i first**, then links the case manually with **Link contract**. | 2026-11-10 |
| 4 | Form order | **Contract first** (it prefills the applicant), then GIRO details. One invoice reference number field. | 2026-11-10 |
| 5 | Applicant | Applicant name / entity, email and mobile are **prefilled from the contract's bill-to** (D16) and can be changed. Name and email mandatory. | 2026-11-10 |
| 6 | Where GIRO is recorded | ~~New table GIRO authorisation~~ (2026-11-10, **replaced 2026-11-11**). The design is **contract › AR-ix case**: the contract has **GIRO status** (Active / Not active) and **Active GIRO case** (reference to the AR-ix case). No separate GIRO table (D21). Since 2026-11-12 AR sets both by hand (decision 11). | 2026-11-11 |
| 9 | One GIRO per contract | A contract has **at most one active GIRO** at any time. *Add GIRO* on a contract with GIRO active is **blocked** (portal and workspace); the requester submits *Remove GIRO* first. Replacing instead of blocking is an open question ([issue #2](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/2)). | 2026-11-11 |
| 10 | Remove GIRO | Ends whatever GIRO is active on the contract (purpose and car plate are recorded on the case but not matched). | 2026-11-11 |
| 7 | Carpark | **One vehicle per form**: car plate, parking start and end dates. | 2026-11-10 |
| 8 | General information | Section 1 is the shared General information variable set (D19): requester name, department, email (SSO, read-only), CC list. | 2026-11-10 |
| 11 | Who changes GIRO on the contract | The AR-ix form is **a request** that signals the project team wants GIRO added or removed; it does not activate GIRO. The AR officer **updates the contract** (GIRO status, Active GIRO case) on the contract record, linking it manually to the AR-ix case, and then uses **Complete** on the case. Complete does **not** check the contract. *Activate GIRO* and *Remove GIRO* are retired. Automating the update from the AR-ix case is for the next major release; the older wording (D21, D22, decisions 6 and 10) is reconciled then ([issue #4](https://github.com/veranzy/ServiceNowARdemo/issues/4)). Open questions 4 answered. | 2026-11-12 |
| 12 | Form sections | **2. Select Customer**: the customer name only, or a new customer (name). **3. Select Contract**: existing, new or no contract (the AR-i options *Existing contract*, *New contract*, *No contract required*). No contract puts the GIRO on the customer's no-contract shell for the requesting department (linked at submit when the shell exists, otherwise AR links it). The applicant stays under Select Contract; GIRO details become section 4. General information unchanged. | 2026-11-12 |
| 13 | Amount | **Amount** (label unchanged) is open **free text**, optional, with no linkages: it doesn't feed the request value or anything else. Open question 3 answered. | 2026-11-12 |
| 14 | Type of request | **Type** (Add GIRO / Remove GIRO) comes first in GIRO details and is set by the contract: **new contract or no contract → Add GIRO only**; **existing contract → Add GIRO if it has no GIRO, Remove GIRO only if GIRO is active**. **Remove GIRO needs no further fields** (attachments stay optional). Help text under Type, owner's wording exactly: "remove-ask payee to contact their bank to terminate, new-please inform payee to make payment until giro is up, officer will contact applicant to complete giro application form and to mail the bank endorsed completed form back". Replaces the validation block of decision 9 with filtered choices; open question 6 answered. | 2026-11-12 |
| 15 | Add GIRO fields | In order: purpose (Rental / Carpark / Others), other purpose if Others; applicant name, email, mobile (no change); **Remarks**. Rental: location (kept) and **Tenancy agreement** (new, optional text), effective start / end date, amount. Carpark: car plate, parking start / end date, amount. Others: **Contract reference number**, **Invoice reference number** (moved here), amount, a second **Remarks**, **Form number** (optional, to be verified, open question 8). Then the attachment section. *Additional details* is no longer on the AR-ix form. Mandatory rules unchanged: purpose, applicant name and email, location and start date (rental), car plate and parking start (carpark). | 2026-11-12 |
| 16 | Attachments and Others | Two new sections after GIRO details, modelled on AR-i: **5. Attachments** (supporting documents only, optional) and **6. Others** (AR-i's hint; **Form number** only, optional, to be verified). Both show for Add and Remove GIRO; Form number shows for every purpose (moved out of the Others purpose block; amends decision 15). | 2026-11-12 |
